Connecting…

Display polls cached telemetry at 4 Hz. The read button requests one fresh observation through the existing connection; it does not arm, stop, recover, clear a fault or set robot parameters.

Waiting for a snapshot…

Recovery works while the torque controller is faulted, as long as the HTTP service and robot connection are available. Pause your policy/setpoint sender first: a new setpoint after recovery can start motion. Manual recovery itself sends no target; with automatic recovery enabled, a new valid setpoint may recover and start control. Franka's recovery operation may move the robot. Keep the workspace clear and physical stop ready. Download diagnostics before clearing the fault.
